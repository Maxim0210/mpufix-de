Common mistakes

Where MPU preparation often goes wrong.

Random videos and forum tips leave gaps, and you may only notice too late that your explanation doesn't hold together.

  • No clear structure
  • Wrong or missing evidence
  • Answers sound memorised
  • The change you describe is hard to believe
Good preparation starts with your actual file and your actual behaviour.

FAQ

What you should know before starting.

Which language is the course in?

The course videos and checklists are in German, and the videos are presented by an AI avatar. Personal advice is available in German, Russian or English. Two videos, including the one on points, are still in production, so ask about the current status before booking if your MPU is about points.

Can anyone guarantee a positive MPU result?

No. Reputable preparation can't promise that. It can help you avoid typical mistakes and explain the changes you have made.

Is online preparation enough?

It can be, if it is structured and fits your file. Complex cases usually need more personal support. Preparation runs online across Germany; an in-person meeting on site is possible by arrangement.

When do I need evidence of abstinence?

That depends on the reason for your MPU, your history and your file. In alcohol, drug and cannabis cases, check this early.
